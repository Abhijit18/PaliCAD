The _split helper in lib/src/csg/boolean.dart is credited as an adaptation
of CSG.Plane.splitPolygon in csg.js, based on its closely matching
classification flags, four output buckets and spanning-edge traversal.
Pali Ijo's version uses Dart vectors and face IDs and adds denominator guards
and clamped intersections. The primary Boolean path uses fragment
classification. Its closed-solid fallback (_Partition and its caller) uses
the BSP clip/invert/build sequence from CSG.js.
